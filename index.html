SNTTI Legacy

Our Journey & Philosophy

Empowering Educators, Transforming Future Generations

Founded in 2016 by Sowmya R., SNTTI was born out of a passionate commitment to fill the gap in quality early childhood teacher training. Over 10+ batches, we have evolved into a center of excellence recognized for practical pedagogy and compassionate educator empowerment.

Founder's Message

"A teacher in early childhood holds the key to a child's confidence, curiosity, and lifelong love for learning. When we empower a woman to become a confident nursery educator, we uplift entire families and schools."

Our Mission
To provide accessible, high-impact teacher training diploma courses that blend child psychology, practical apparatus usage, and modern classroom management.
Our Vision
To become the benchmark institute across South India for early childhood teacher training, fostering an alumni network of 1,000+ top preschool educators.
